## Implemented boundary

Five pure functions construct complete inspection, trust approve/revoke and target
select/revoke originals. A privately constructed object-reference value retains
canonical arrays, reuses the existing original codec grammar and redacts reflection.
Builders revalidate typed context/catalogue/source/target/artifact snapshots, compare
their exact bases, join imports to manifest roles and preserve explicit operation
IDs, counters, nulls and historical inspection references. Approval refuses an
already-approved action even when its author is unavailable. Target replacement
may change artifact; private physical continuity remains Store-owned.

The builder owns no credential, socket, clock, randomness, source checkout or
pending publication. It grants no current authorization. The app assembly source
list includes it; selected guided driver/form composition remains separate work.
