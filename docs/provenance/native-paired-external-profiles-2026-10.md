## Evidence limits and next delivery

NPE-T1/T2 and bounded portions of T3/T4/T5 have executable metadata, actual
transport/Store/private-file and unsigned app evidence. Positive production
signed acquisition, five-second selected-to-original publication/delivery and its
late-publication races require the actual installed app/Keychain environment.
Raw-key peers and compiled code do not complete that acceptance. Installed
accessibility, qualified protocol publishers/identity, power-loss survival and
physical source qualification remain. This slice changes no schema, producer API,
dependency, sibling repository, source qualification or default dispatch guard.
