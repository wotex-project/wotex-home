## Implemented boundary

NativeGenerationClient reads exact policy metadata, reconstructs domain-separated
provider/policy SHA, forwards unchanged originals once, checks full reservation/
status correspondence and cancels a preparation by its original identity. Its
result codec preserves complete inert assessments/reports/diagnostics and refuses
contradictory states, malformed values and byte/tree/collection/text excess.
Historical decoding needs no model or producer. The generation-only integer bridge
accepts exact native Swift Int64 without widening older shared scalar decoders.

The local original-purpose runner repeats original custody/controller/principal
and revision guards. Running and unsettled unknown stay pending. Unknown is a
knowledge snapshot: a worker can exit after sending a result while Store has not
processed it. Explicit acknowledgeUnknown performs existing-only status and exact
journal CAS after the panel's confirmation dialog; it sends no new attempt.
Candidate mechanics and cancellation remain separate from source review, approval,
physical success and proof that a remote model stopped.

The app and affected fixture compiler closures include the SDK. The macOS CI job
now registers generation-original, full SDK and pending-coordinator checks.
Registration is not evidence of a new hosted green run; nothing was pushed.
